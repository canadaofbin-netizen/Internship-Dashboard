# Target Research Template

This template is designed to analyze and reverse-engineer potential targets (Academic Labs, Tech Companies, Startups, and Individual Researchers) to find the best intersection with your profile.

## 1. Overview & General Information
- **Name of Target** (Lab / Company / Startup / Researcher)
- **Type** (Academic Lab / Industry / Startup)
- **Location / Institution**
- **Key Focus Area** (e.g., Neuroscience, AR/VR, Wearables, AI, HCI, On-device ML)
- **Key Contacts / Principal Investigators (PIs) / Managers** (Include specific email addresses, prioritizing university emails if applicable).

## 2. Core Research & Technology
- **Main Research Topics**: What are the primary problems they are trying to solve? (e.g., mechanisms of hallucinations, HCI, On-device AI, neuromotor interfaces).
- **Key Technologies & Methods**: What tools and hardware do they rely on? (e.g., M/EEG, fNIRS, Machine Learning, Graph Neural Networks, Edge AI).
- **Data & Scale**: What kind of data do they handle? (e.g., large-scale non-invasive EEG, wearable device-derived metrics, physiological monitoring).
- **Recent Projects / Papers**: Notable recent works that show their current trajectory.

## 3. Fit & Intersection with My Profile
- **Target Bottlenecks**: What technical challenges are they likely facing? (e.g., noisy multi-channel EEG data, integrating heavy lab equipment with portable wearables, optimizing ML parameters for low-power devices).
- **My Core Competencies to Highlight**:
  - BCI / EEG Signal Processing (Pipeline optimization, channel reduction, denoising)
  - Machine Learning Optimization / Edge AI / Data Science
  - Wearable sensors & Smart Glasses integration
  - UCL PALS background (understanding the biological/psychological/HCI context)
- **Specific Synergy (Tailored Contribution Point)**: How can my skills directly contribute to their goals? (e.g., "Building an automated ML pipeline to reduce dimensionality in their wearable EEG data," "On-device ML parameter optimization").
